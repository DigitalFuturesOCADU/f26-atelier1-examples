iPhone Permission Order

Does the order of one tap's requests decide what an iPhone allows? For the Class 11 AR examples.

Open each test in a new tab, in this order. Answer Allow to every question the phone asks. Each test says what worked. Then copy the results into the chat.

  1. camera, then motion
    The old order of enableARTap. Expected on an iPhone: no motion.
  2. sound, then motion, then camera
    Use AR in Your Project, step 7, Level 1.
  3. motion, then camera
    01 Look Around and 02 Tap to Place after the fix.
  4. sound, then camera
    Use AR in Your Project, step 7, Level 2 (the creature with a beep).

An iPhone may remember motion for the site. If it does, a later test gets motion without asking, and says so.
