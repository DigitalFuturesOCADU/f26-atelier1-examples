Your Room

A room is just a name. Phones that use the same room meet, and phones with a different room never do. Type the room you were given. This page then gives you every Share Basic opened in your room, a QR code for each, and a Room Inspector that watches it.

Small letters, numbers and dashes. Spaces become dashes. This browser remembers it.

The Share Basics, in your room

Open one on a laptop, then scan its QR code with a phone. Or scan it with two phones. The Inspector link shows everything the phones in that room send.

Your own sketch, in your room

Put your room in shareSetup(). Give your sketch its own app name, so it never meets the Basics in the same room.


    
Watch your own sketch in the Room Inspector (app my-project. Change app= in the address if yours is different.)


    
In the p5.js Web Editor

    
The Web Editor does not pass ?room= on to a sketch. Put your room in the ROOM line at the top of sketch.js instead, and save. The QR code the sketch shows on a laptop opens it on a phone.
