Room Inspector

Watch a Share room: who is in it, what each phone sends, what the room shares, and every event with who sent it. It joins the room as one more page, with no name, so the Share Basics leave it out.

The room and the app must match the sketch you want to watch. Each Share Basic names its app, such as send-a-number. A sketch with no app is in default.
