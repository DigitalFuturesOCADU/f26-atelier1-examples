Class 5 · Making Sound

Seven small examples, one way of making sound each: a recording, a changed recording, an echo, a tone, a loop, an instrument and speech. Touch only. Tap once to turn on sound.

  • 01 Play a Recording codep5.sound. Load a recording and play it. Each tap starts it again.
    Touch
  • 02 Bend a Recording codep5.sound. A loop. Left and right is the speed, and the pitch moves with it. Up and down is the volume.
    Touch
  • 03 Echo a Recording codep5.sound. The bell goes through an echo, p5.Delay. Where you tap sets the time between repeats and how many.
    Touch
  • 04 Make a Tone codep5.sound. An oscillator. Hold a finger down: left is low, right is high. Nothing was recorded.
    Touch
  • 05 Start a Loop codeTone.js. Eight notes loop on Tone's own clock. Drag to set the tempo.
    Touch
  • 06 Play an Instrument codesmplr. A sampled marimba. Tap a key to strike that note.
    Touch
  • 07 Say the Time codeSpeech. The phone's own voice says the time. Where you tap sets how fast and how high.
    Touch
